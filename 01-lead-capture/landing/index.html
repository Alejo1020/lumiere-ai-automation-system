FAQ

Good questions

How much do treatments cost?

Pricing depends on your goals and the treatment plan. As a reference, Botox starts at $13 per unit and HydraFacials at $199. You'll get a transparent, itemized quote at your free consultation — and members save up to 20%.

It's my first time. What should I expect?

Your first visit is a 30-minute conversation with a licensed specialist: we listen to your goals, assess your skin and recommend a plan. There's no obligation to book a treatment that day.

Does it hurt? Is there downtime?

Most treatments feel like a quick pinch or warmth, and we use numbing cream when needed. HydraFacial and Botox have no downtime; fillers and microneedling may cause mild redness or swelling for 1–3 days.

Where are you located?

We're in Old Town Scottsdale at 7150 E Via Soleri Dr, Suite 204, with free covered parking in the building.
